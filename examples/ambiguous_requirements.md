# Ambiguous scenario: shorten links for a public campaign

Build a short-link service for a public marketing campaign. It should be fast, scalable, secure, and provide analytics. Stakeholders expect it to work globally.

## Intentionally unresolved decisions
- No traffic estimate, latency objective, availability SLO, or geographic distribution is provided.
- Authentication and authorization requirements are unspecified.
- Analytics dimensions, retention, privacy rules, and bot filtering are unspecified.
- It is unclear whether links need custom aliases, expiration, editing, or deactivation.

The requirement analyst should record these as questions and assumptions. Do not silently claim production readiness or invent numeric SLOs. A human should resolve material questions before implementation or production promotion.
